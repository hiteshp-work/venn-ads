B2B LinkedIn Document Ad Architecture

Designed to target the 50–200 mid-market CFO and finance decision-maker segment, driving qualified demo pipeline while educating SMB founders on the 2% interest yield.

Campaign Objective:
Lead Generation (Native Form) + Engagement Document Ad
Target Audience Filters (Canada):
• Job Titles: CFO, VP Finance, Director of Finance, Controller, Head of Finance, Founder, Managing Director
• Company Size: 11–50 and 51–200 employees (Mid-Market focus)
• Geography: Canada (ON, BC, AB, QC metro centers)
• Member Groups: Financial Executives International Canada (FEI), Canadian CPA Network
Conversion Strategy (LinkedIn Dual-CTA Architecture):

LinkedIn feed ads allow only one native CTA button per ad unit. To deliver both "See the platform" and the gated "Interest Calculator" without losing attribution, we structure the flow across 3 touchpoints:

• Touchpoint 1 (Feed CTA Button): Primary native button opens the pre-filled LinkedIn Lead Gen Form ("Get the Calculator").
• Touchpoint 2 (Post Ad Copy): A clean, contextual link directly in the primary text ("venn.ca/platform →") captures immediate high-intent clickers who skip the form.
• Touchpoint 3 (Form Thank-You Screen): Upon submitting the form, LinkedIn's native confirmation screen presents the mandatory redirect button: "See the platform →".
